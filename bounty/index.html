How it works

1. Event password

Players include the password in their drop screenshots to prove the drop happened during the event. Press 🎲 to roll a new one or ✎ to type your own.

2. Add bounties

  • Type a boss name and press Enter to load its drop table from the OSRS Wiki. Click a drop to add it as an active bounty.
  • Click ☆ next to a drop to favourite it. The ☆ button under the list switches between Standard (the current boss) and Curated (all your favourites).
  • Random Bounty picks a drop from the current list, respecting the Hide filters and the rarity range. Tick Prevent duplicates to skip drops that are already active.
  • Type anything in the custom bounty box (e.g. "Any Unique") and press ＋.
  • Use ▾ → Create new collection to build your own list of drops. Search its name like a boss.

3. Claim bounties

Select a bounty, type the claimant's name, then choose Claim and Remove (one-time bounty) or Claim without Removing (bounty stays up for others). Claims appear on the left. Select one and press Delete Completed to remove it.

4. Streaming

Zoom In shows a clean full-screen view of the bounties and password for screen sharing.

Everything is saved in your browser, so refreshing the page won't lose your event.
