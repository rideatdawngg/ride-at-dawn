Who We Are

Ride Together. Rise Together.

RAD is a PvM-focused variety clan built on real friendship. Whether you're greenlogging your first raid or chasing a purple, there's a seat at the table and someone ready to teach.

🗡️

Raids & PvM

Weekly ToB, ToA and CoX runs, Boss of the Week, and masses. Experienced raiders and first-timers in the same calls.

📜

Learner Friendly

Dedicated learner groups and patient teachers. Nobody gets kicked for a death — we'd rather explain the mechanic.

🏆

Competitions

SOTW, BOTW, power hours and quarterly clan Bingo with real prizes and balanced teams.

🛡️

Clan Progression

Eight ranks from Trialist to Legend. XP keeps you active — participation ranks you up.

🎙️

Voice & Community

Active Discord with voice channels, event pings, drop shares and plenty of banter between runs.

🤝

Brotherhood

Respect, encouragement and no favoritism. Real life comes first, and the clan will be here when you log back in.

Clan Progression

Rank Breakdown

Our progression ranks reward members for participating in events, competitions, and the RAD community.

⭐ How to Earn Points

⚔️ Attend a clan event5
🎉 Host a clan event10
⛏️ Participate in SOTW5
💀 Participate in BOTW5
🥉 Place 3rd in SOTW/BOTW+5
🥈 Place 2nd in SOTW/BOTW+7
🥇 Place 1st in SOTW/BOTW+10
🤝 Make a clear community contribution5

📜 Important Information

  • Points are lifetime points and never expire.
  • There is no point decay for taking a break.
  • Rank-ups happen automatically when you reach the required threshold.
  • XP does not increase your progression rank — participation does.
  • Leadership positions are separate from progression ranks.

How We Run

Leadership

Leaders are held to the same standard as every member. Issues are handled privately, fairly, and after hearing both sides.

Leaders

Set the direction of the clan and have final say on major decisions.

Council

Reviews applications, organizes events, monitors chat and supports leadership.

Raid Leaders

Run raid nights, organize learner groups, and teach mechanics.

Chat Moderators

Keep clan chat and Discord friendly, welcoming and on track.
